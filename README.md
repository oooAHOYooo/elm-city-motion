# Elm City Motion

Standalone editorial website for Elm City Motion, a film and video studio in New Haven, Connecticut.

## Preview locally

```sh
python3 -m http.server 4173
```

Then visit `http://127.0.0.1:4173`.

## Motion & Photography Assets

Production and location assets for New Haven backgrounds are stored in `assets/`:
- `assets/nh-koffee-motion.mp4`: Lightweight (237 KB) black-and-white silent looping background video powering the bottom contact section (DaVinci Resolve client shoot)
- `assets/nh-koffee-motion.gif`: Lightweight (1 MB) black-and-white 10fps looping web GIF fallback
- `assets/nh-bts-camera.jpg`: Production set — Cinema camera rig on tripod & directing in dramatic chiaroscuro (Hero slide 01)
- `assets/nh-koffee-counter.jpg`: Koffee? on Audubon St — Barista motion blur & pastry counter (Hero slide 02)
- `assets/nh-koffee-interior.jpg`: Koffee? on Audubon St — Interior wide shot, chandelier & brick arches (Hero slide 03 & About backdrop)
- `assets/nh-koffee-espresso.jpg`: Koffee? on Audubon St — Espresso bar, brick wall & clock (Hero slide 04)
- `assets/nh-koffee-cup.jpg`: Koffee? on Audubon St — Ceramic cup, latte & chocolate cookies (Hero slide 05)
- `assets/nh-koffee-sticker.jpg`: Koffee? on Audubon St — Illustrative brand sticker on woodgrain (Hero slide 06)
- `assets/ecm-hero.png`: Classic downtown street corner at blue hour

## Wordmarks

`assets/ecm-wordmark.svg` is the white hero version, with a subtle motion exposure behind the second line. `assets/ecm-wordmark-print.svg` is the crisp one-color black version for print, festival laurels, and credits.
